- Nature: `/private/tmp/painted-backdrops.QOx69u/nature.png`. Sparse left tree and right cherry blossom branch, tiny outer-bottom path strokes and rocks. Center and top strip clear.

Shared limitation: all four visibly use pale ivory ground with subtle texture/color variation, rather than the exact uniform #fbfaf6 requested. Edge figures are opaque and clearly defined. No people, dogs, tables, desks, chairs, foreground stages or central objects appear.
