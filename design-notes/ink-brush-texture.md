## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: one 1024x1024 square seamless tileable grayscale material texture for tinting onto 3D objects in a minimalist modern Korean painting portfolio.

Primary request: neutral grayscale dry-brush Korean ink-wash material texture on silk. The entire canvas is filled evenly with overlapping broad translucent ink washes, clearly visible fine directional bristle streaks, sparse dry-brush white gaps, and subtle woven silk tooth.

Style/medium: a flat material map with actual broad brush gestures and fine fibers.

Color palette: grayscale only; medium-gray values varied with white bristle flecks and darker brush-grain; evenly distributed medium contrast; no large black ink blobs. The map will later be tinted indigo, celadon, or rose by shaders.

Composition/framing: seamless tileable edges, even coverage across the whole square, no central focal point.

Lighting: flat diffuse lighting, no shadows.

Avoid: scene, illustration, paper photograph, UI, vignette, text, letters, calligraphy, symbols, logos, border, objects, generic random noise, concrete, wood grain, marble, or crumpled paper.

Generate exactly one raster image.

## Motion treatment

The existing Korean-name mark traces itself with dry-brush pigment. The circular stage turns in three dimensions. Each centered scene reveals with a rough-edged diagonal brush sweep: an ivory underpainting and thin contour lead, followed by the color wash. Character motion and the sentence bubble wait until the paint has settled. Coffee, working, park with two dogs, and standing watering retain their existing content and animation routines. The texture is attached to object coordinates, so it moves with the forms rather than sliding over the screen. Reduced motion shows finished scenes immediately.
